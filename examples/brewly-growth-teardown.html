The headline result. Pause here.
Nobody gets excited about a reward that's a month away. Make the next win feel close.

Maya Chen, co-founder of Brewly (fictional) 21:47

This is the quote to remember. It generalizes beyond coffee.

Why it worked: the goal-gradient effect

People speed up as a goal gets closer. Three short goals beat one long one, and surprise rewards make each visit a little lottery. 🎰

This is our interpretation, not the founder's words. Mention variable rewards too.

What to steal

Three moves you can copy this week

🎯

Shrink the goal

Make the first win reachable in days, not weeks.

📊

Show progress

A ring or bar that says "1 more to go".

🎁

Surprise them

Rotate small perks so rewards feel fresh.

Ask the audience where their product has a "month-away reward".
